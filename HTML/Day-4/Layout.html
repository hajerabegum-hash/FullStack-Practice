DUM BRIYANI

INGREDIENT
  • RICE 2KG
  • BEEF 2KG
  • CURD 1KG
  • VEGETABLES AS PER QUANTITY
  • SPICIES AS PER QUANTITY

  1. Take a pan add oil and fry the sliced onion
  2. Take another container and make a mixture of beef by adding curd,spices &vegetables put it on stove ( low flame)
  3. on another stove put soaked rice until get boiled
  4. then strain the water from rice and add the layed of mixture betwwen the rice
  5. put it on stove for 30min
  6. READY TO SERVE
