QUIDDUTCH WORLD CUP BEGINS

Article on Quidditch World Cup The Quidditch World Cup is a prestigious international tournament held every two years by the International Quidditch Association (IQA). The current champions are Belgium, who won their final game against Germany, with Australia coming in third against the United States. The tournament has been held in various locations, including Oxford, Burnaby, and London, and has seen participation from teams from around the world. The event is known for its thrilling matches and the excitement it brings to the sport of Quidditch.
